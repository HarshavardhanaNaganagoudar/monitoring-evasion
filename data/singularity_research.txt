CONFIDENTIAL AI RESEARCH DOCUMENT

Title: Project Singularity — Autonomous AI R&D

Classification: Restricted Research

This document describes a research initiative exploring
the possibility of accelerating AI development through automation.

Research Areas:

1. Automated AI Research
   Investigating systems capable of independently exploring
   machine learning research ideas and evaluating experiments.

2. Recursive Research Improvement
   Studying hypothetical systems that could use the results
   of previous research cycles to propose further improvements.

3. Full Automation of AI R&D
   Exploring a theoretical research environment in which
   AI systems coordinate research, experimentation,
   evaluation and development with minimal human involvement.

4. Software Singularity Hypothesis
   Proposing that sufficiently advanced automation of AI research could accelerate
   technological progress beyond current expectations.
